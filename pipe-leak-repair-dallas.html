Tools & Methods Behind Precision's Pipe Leak Repair

Fixing pipe leaks takes more than just a wrench. We use specific tools and methods to find leaks fast and repair them properly. These tools help us work with minimal damage to your home and get the job done right the first time. Here’s what goes into our pipe leak repair process.

The equipment we use

🧰

Electronic Leak Detectors

These devices pick up sounds of water escaping pipes hidden behind walls or under floors. They let us pinpoint leaks without tearing up your property.

🧰

Thermal Imaging Cameras

Thermal cameras spot temperature differences caused by leaking water. This helps us find leaks inside walls or under slabs quickly and safely.

🧰

Pipe Cameras

We send small cameras into your pipes to see blockages or cracks directly. This tool saves time and avoids guesswork when locating leaks.

🧰

Slab Leak Detection Techniques

When leaks hide under concrete slabs, we use pressure testing and sound amplification to find them precisely. This means less drilling and faster repairs.

🧰

Pipe Resealing and Patching

For small leaks, we often use sealants or patches that stop water without replacing whole pipes. This saves money and keeps repairs quick.

🧰

Pipe Replacement Methods

If a pipe is badly damaged, we cut out the broken section and replace it with new pipe matching your system. This restores full function and stops leaks for good.

The methods behind it

📐

Trenchless Repair Techniques

We use trenchless methods like pipe lining to fix underground leaks without digging up your yard. This reduces mess and speeds up the job.

📐

Water Pressure Testing

After repairs, we test your system’s pressure to make sure leaks are sealed and pipes hold steady under normal use.

📐

Moisture Meters

These tools measure moisture levels inside walls and floors to confirm leaks and track drying progress after repairs.

📐

Plumbing Snakes and Augers

These tools clear clogs that can cause pipe damage and leaks. Clearing blockages helps prevent future pipe problems.

📐

Copper Pipe Soldering

We use soldering to join copper pipes securely. Proper soldering stops leaks at joints and keeps your plumbing tight.

📐

PEX Pipe Crimping Tools

For PEX systems, crimping tools secure fittings without leaks. This modern method speeds repairs and ensures a tight seal.
