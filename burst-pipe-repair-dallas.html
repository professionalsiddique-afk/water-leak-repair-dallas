How the work gets done

Tools & Methods Behind Precision Leak's Burst Pipe Repair

Fixing a burst pipe means more than just replacing a pipe section. We use specific tools and methods that help us find hidden leaks, cut out damaged pipes, and restore your plumbing safely. These tools let us work precisely and avoid extra damage.

The equipment we use

🧰

Electronic Leak Detectors to pinpoint hidden bursts

These devices pick up the sound of water escaping from pipes buried in walls or under slabs. Spotting the exact leak location saves time and reduces the need for tearing up your home unnecessarily.

🧰

Pipe Cutting Tools for clean, precise repairs

We use specialized pipe cutters to remove only the damaged sections. Clean cuts help ensure tight joints when we install new pipes, preventing future leaks and giving your system a long-lasting fix.

🧰

Pipe Threading Machines for secure connections

When replacing metal pipes, threading machines create screw-on ends that form strong seals. This method is preferred for durability and to avoid leaks at joints, especially in older plumbing systems.

🧰

Video Pipe Inspection Cameras to check pipe interiors

We snake cameras through your pipes to see inside without digging. This helps us spot cracks, corrosion, or blockages that might have caused the burst, ensuring a thorough repair plan.

🧰

PEX Crimping Tools for flexible pipe repairs

PEX piping is common in Dallas homes. Crimping tools secure fittings quickly and tightly, making repairs faster and reducing the chance of leaks in these flexible systems.

The methods behind it

📐

Pipe Freezing Technique to isolate sections

This method freezes water inside a pipe, creating an ice plug. It stops water flow so we can repair or replace a section without shutting off water to the whole house, minimizing disruption.

📐

Hydrostatic Pressure Testing to ensure leak-free repairs

After fixing a burst pipe, we pressurize the system with water to check for leaks. This test confirms that all connections hold tight before restoring water service to your home.

📐

Slip Couplings to join pipes in tight spaces

These special fittings slide over the pipe ends and seal leaks without needing to remove long pipe sections. They’re useful for quick fixes in hard-to-reach spots.

📐

Thermal Imaging Cameras to detect moisture behind walls

Thermal cameras spot cold spots from leaking water inside walls and ceilings. This non-invasive tool helps us find burst pipes early and plan repairs with minimal damage to your home.

📐

Soldering Torches for copper pipe repairs

When working with copper pipes, soldering ensures strong, leak-proof joints. Proper soldering technique is key to a lasting repair and to prevent future bursts at the joints.
